## Our Project Process
1. **Discovery Call** — We discuss your goals, target audience, and project scope. This is a free, no-obligation consultation.
2. **Proposal & Quote** — We send a detailed proposal with scope, timeline, and cost breakdown within 2-3 business days.
3. **Design Phase** — We create wireframes and visual mockups. You get 2 rounds of revisions included.
4. **Development Phase** — Our developers build the approved design with clean, optimized code. You receive weekly progress updates.
5. **Testing & QA** — We test across browsers, devices, and screen sizes. We also run speed and SEO checks.
6. **Launch** — We deploy to your hosting, configure DNS, and ensure everything runs smoothly.
7. **Post-Launch Support** — We provide 30 days of free bug-fix support after launch. After that, you can choose a maintenance plan.

## Revision & Refund Policy
- **Revisions during a project**: You receive 2 rounds of design revisions included in every project. Additional revision rounds are billed at $75/hour.
- **Revisions on maintenance plans**: Revisions are covered within your monthly hour allocation.
- **Refund policy**: If the project has not entered the development phase, you may request a full refund of any deposit paid. Once development has begun, refunds are prorated based on work completed. Maintenance plan subscriptions can be cancelled anytime with 30 days notice; no refunds for the current billing period.
- **Satisfaction guarantee**: If you are not satisfied with the final deliverable, we will work with you to make it right at no additional cost, provided feedback is given within 14 days of delivery.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to build a website?
A typical business website takes 3–4 weeks from kickoff to launch. More complex projects like eCommerce stores or web applications may take 6–12 weeks depending on the features required.

### What platform do you build websites on?
We primarily work with WordPress, Shopify, React, and Next.js. We recommend the best platform based on your specific needs — for example, Shopify for eCommerce, WordPress for content-heavy sites, and React/Next.js for custom web applications.

### Do you provide website hosting?
We do not provide hosting directly, but we help you set up reliable hosting on platforms like Vercel, AWS, SiteGround, or Shopify's built-in hosting. We handle the full deployment process for you.

### Can I update the website myself after launch?
Yes! For WordPress and Shopify sites, we provide a brief training session and a user guide so you can make basic content updates yourself. For more technical changes, our maintenance plans cover that.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, credit/debit cards, and PayPal. For projects over $2,000, we offer a 50/50 payment split — 50% upfront and 50% upon completion.

### Do you sign NDAs?
Yes, we are happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement before starting any project. Confidentiality is important to us.

### What happens if I need changes after the project is delivered?
You have 14 days after delivery to request changes covered under our satisfaction guarantee. After that, changes are billed at our standard hourly rate or covered under a maintenance plan.

### Do you offer a free consultation?
Yes, we offer a free 30-minute discovery call to discuss your project goals and provide initial recommendations. No obligation.
